# Code of Conduct

ASRbox follows a simple contributor covenant: be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Give actionable technical feedback.
- Assume good intent, especially across languages and time zones.
- Keep issue and PR discussions relevant to ASRbox.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.
- Bad-faith reports or intentionally misleading technical claims.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; block users; or restrict participation when behavior harms the project community.

If you see a problem, contact the maintainer through GitHub.
